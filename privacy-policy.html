Dancelcar Salted Eggs Enterprise ("we," "us," "our," or "the Enterprise") operates the Dancelcar Salted Eggs Enterprise mobile application (the "Application") and associated web/API services hosted at https://dancelcarsaltedegg.com .

We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting personal and business information in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), directives of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is processed and safeguarded, the limited circumstances under which it is shared, and your rights as a data subject when using our Application.

1. Scope and Target Audience

The Application is a business-to-business (B2B) supply chain, wholesale egg procurement, inventory management, and delivery dispatch platform designed exclusively for commercial use in the Republic of the Philippines.

User Roles

  1. Business Owner: Oversees product lines, sets pricing, approves commercial accounts, audits compliance records, and manages supply procurement and vendor fulfillment.
  2. Suppliers (Egg Producers / Distributors): Maintain egg inventories, manage wholesale purchase orders, and fulfill deliveries.
  3. Vendors (Retailers / Commercial Buyers): Browse wholesale egg inventories, place orders, upload payment receipts, and schedule delivery drop-offs.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to authenticate commercial partners, facilitate inventory transactions, dispatch agricultural deliveries, and comply with Philippine business regulations.

A. Account Registration and Profile Information

During registration and profile setup, we collect:

  • Personal Details: First name and last name.
  • Enterprise Details: Business name and commercial classification/type.
  • Contact Information: Active email address and contact telephone/mobile number.
  • Physical Address: House/building number, street, barangay, city/municipality, province, and ZIP code.
  • Credentials: Account password, which is cryptographically hashed on our server and never stored or readable in plaintext.
  • Profile Avatar: Optional profile picture or company logo uploaded from your device.

B. Regulatory and Business Verification Documents

To prevent fraud, guarantee food supply chain integrity, and verify legitimate commercial operations, users are required during onboarding to upload copies of legitimate commercial licenses:

  • Mayor's / Business Permit
  • Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Registration Certificate
  • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)

These documents are reviewed strictly by the System Owner for commercial onboarding and compliance auditing.

C. Order, Inventory, and Transaction Data

When conducting transactions through the Application, we record:

  • Egg order specifications, including grades, sizes, tray/crate quantities, unit costs, and total amounts.
  • Batch, stock level, and inventory adjustment records.
  • Order status histories, including pending, approved, in-transit, delivered, and cancelled.
  • Return and damage claims, including reported quantities of cracked or spoiled eggs, return reasons, and optional photographic proof.

D. Payment and Financial Information

  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • Walk-in Cash
  • GCash

When paying via GCash, buyers may upload a screenshot or photo of their GCash transaction receipt to verify payment.

Suppliers and Owners may configure their designated GCash account name, GCash mobile number, and optional GCash payment QR code.

Important Financial Notice: We do NOT collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account credentials, CVV codes, or online banking passwords. All digital payments are conducted externally through the user's independent GCash mobile wallet.

E. Geolocation Data

Delivery Pinpoint — Single Read

When a Vendor submits an order, the Application requests foreground access to device coordinates (latitude and longitude) using high accuracy for a maximum of 5 seconds.

This captures the delivery destination coordinate to assist delivery drivers in finding the warehouse or store.

Active Delivery Tracking — Streaming Read

When a delivery driver or Supplier is actively fulfilling an order marked as "In Transit," the Application reads live GPS coordinates, updated approximately every 10 meters, exclusively while the tracking screen is actively open.

No Background Tracking

We do not run persistent background location tracking services. GPS access terminates immediately when the order is placed, when the delivery tracking screen is closed, or when the Application is minimized or terminated.

F. Technical and Device Data

  • IP address and network connectivity state.
  • Custom User-Agent identifier: DancelcarApp/1.0.
  • Session cookies (PHPSESSID).
  • Authorization Bearer tokens.
  • Anti-CSRF tokens.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data based on legitimate business interests, contractual necessity, and compliance with legal obligations under Philippine law.

  1. Account Verification: Verify commercial identity, assess legal registration documents, and approve business accounts.
  2. Order Fulfillment: Record purchase orders, reserve inventory in real time, calculate subtotals, and arrange dispatch.
  3. Delivery Logistics: Compute road routes and estimated delivery travel times, and display interactive delivery maps.
  4. Payment Reconciliation: Verify uploaded GCash payment receipts against pending order totals.
  5. Quality Assurance & Returns: Review damaged egg claims, manage refunds or product replacements, and audit supplier quality consistency.
  6. Platform Security & Account Recovery: Authenticate identity, prevent unauthorized access, and deliver 6-digit One-Time Passwords (OTP) for password recovery.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Data collected by the Application is disclosed solely to the parties required to execute wholesale commercial transactions.

Between Transaction Counterparts

A Vendor's business name, contact number, and delivery address are shared with the fulfilling Supplier or Enterprise Driver solely to execute order delivery.

Enterprise Administration

Uploaded compliance documents, including DTI, BIR, and Mayor's Permit documents, and transaction dispute details are accessible only to authorized System Owners and compliance administrators.

Legal Obligations

We may disclose information if required by applicable Philippine laws, valid court orders, or subpoenas issued by law enforcement or regulatory authorities such as the National Privacy Commission (NPC) or Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR).

6. Data Storage, Security, and Local Session Retention

Data in Transit

All communications between the Application and our backend servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security / Secure Sockets Layer (HTTPS).

Data at Rest

Sensitive server databases are protected behind firewalls, database authentication credentials, and strict role-based access controls. Passwords are salted and hashed using secure algorithms prior to database storage.

Local Storage on Device

The Application uses Android SharedPreferences to locally store:

  • User profile session object.
  • User ID, name, email, role, and business name.
  • Authentication Bearer token.
  • PHP Session Identifier (PHPSESSID).

When you tap "Logout" in the Application, all local session tokens and cached user profile data are purged from your device immediately.

8. Your Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173)

As a registered data subject under Philippine law, you are entitled to the following rights:

  1. Right to be Informed: Know whether personal data is being processed, how it was obtained, and the purposes for processing.
  2. Right to Access: Request access to personal information stored in our databases.
  3. Right to Rectification: Dispute inaccuracies and request correction of personal information.
  4. Right to Erasure or Blocking: Request removal, suspension, or withdrawal of personal information under lawful conditions.
  5. Right to Damages: Claim compensation for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal data.
  6. Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) .
